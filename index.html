How it works

The Encoder is the reverse of the Decoder. It reads a JSON or CSV file (exported from the Decoder tab), re-encodes each account into the Google Authenticator protobuf binary format, base64-encodes it, and wraps it inside an otpauth-migration://offline?data=… URI. That URI is rendered as a scannable QR code PNG.

If you have many accounts, they are automatically split into batches of 10 to keep each QR code scannable. Scan all generated QR codes in order to import every account.

Privacy: Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is transmitted.
